This Privacy Policy explains how Cyborg Noodle (referred to as "the Bot", "we", or "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects your data. The Bot is an instance of the open-source [nadeko-bot/nadekobot](https://github.com/nadeko-bot/nadekobot) project with cosmetic theming customizations, hosted independently by the administrators of the Gorillaz Fan Discord server. The Bot operates only in this server.

By interacting with the server while the Bot is present, you consent to the data practices described in this policy. This policy is designed to comply with the [Discord Developer Terms of Service](https://support-dev.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/8562894815383-Discord-Developer-Terms-of-Service) and [Developer Policy](https://support-dev.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/8563934450327-Discord-Developer-Policy).

## 1. Data We Collect

The Bot collects and stores the following information:

1. **User Data:** Your Discord User ID, username, and avatar.
2. **Activity Statistics:** Counts of your messages and time spent in voice channels, used to calculate XP and levels. The Bot counts that you sent a message; it does not log what the message said.
3. **Currency Data:** Your Bananaz balance and transaction history from claims, games, and gifts.
4. **Moderation Records:** Warnings issued to you (including the reason written by the moderator), and records of mutes, kicks, and bans, kept as the server's infraction history.
5. **Command-Submitted Content:** Text you deliberately submit through Bot commands, such as quotes, reminders, or game inputs.
6. **Server Data:** Server and channel IDs needed for the Bot's configuration.

**Deleted and edited messages:** For moderation purposes, when a message is deleted or edited, the Bot posts the original content to a staff-only log channel within Discord. This allows the moderation team to review rule-breaking messages that a user deletes or edits to avoid enforcement. This content remains on Discord's platform as messages in that channel; it is not written to the Bot's database.

**What we do not collect:** Outside of the deleted/edited message log described above, the Bot reads chat messages in real time to respond to commands, trigger currency drops, award XP, and enforce moderation filters, but it does not log or store the content of general chat messages.

## 2. How We Store Your Data

All collected data is stored off-platform in a local database on hardware operated by the server's Bot administrator. The storage volume is encrypted at rest, and access to the machine and database is limited to the Bot administrator.

## 3. How We Use Your Data

The data we collect is used only to provide the Bot's functionality:

1. **Moderation:** Maintaining an accurate infraction history so staff can enforce server rules fairly and consistently.
2. **XP and Currency Systems:** Tracking activity to award XP, levels, role rewards, and Bananaz.
3. **Server Features:** Greetings, anti-raid protection, chat games, and event theming.

## 4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, monetize, or trade your data. Your data is accessible only to:

1. **Authorized Server Staff:** Moderators and administrators of the Gorillaz Fan server, through the Bot's commands (e.g. viewing a user's warning history).
2. **The Bot Administrator:** The individual responsible for hosting the Bot and its database.

We will not share your data with any third parties unless required by law or to comply with Discord Trust & Safety requests.

## 5. Data Retention

Moderation records, XP, and currency data are retained indefinitely to preserve a consistent infraction history and keep the server's progression systems working. Data for users who leave the server is not automatically deleted, so that infraction history survives leave/rejoin cycles.

## 6. Your Rights and Data Deletion

Under the Discord Developer Policies, you have the right to request deletion of your personal data. To have your data removed from the Bot's database, contact:

1. Any member of the server's moderation team; or
2. The server owner; or
3. The Bot administrator listed below.

Upon receiving a valid request, we will remove your identifying information from our database within 30 days. Note that we may retain moderation records in anonymized form where needed for ongoing rule enforcement.

## 7. Contact Information

For questions, concerns, or deletion requests:

1. Discord: oceanbreeze (Bot administrator)
2. Server staff: reach any moderator via the server
